# Vision – decentralized, AI-assisted excursions (V1)

**Artifact ID:** EXK-CONCEPT-01

A local Facebook community publishes compelling destinations in and around Göttingen. Unlike a conventional club, it has no permanent excursion leader. A volunteer or other suitable member can offer to lead one specific outing; the AI administrator helps that person plan and announce it.

## Desired outcome
- Ongoing discovery of destinations: farms, workshops, natural areas, technical facilities, art, culture, history.
- Every suggested trip has an invitation: **"Who would like to lead this excursion?"**
- A candidate contacts the group administrator through **Facebook Messenger or a Facebook comment**.
- ChatGPT Work asks the relevant questions and prepares a short assessment of fit, feasibility and missing arrangements.
- Once the guide and arrangements are confirmed, Work announces a concrete event in the Facebook group and coordinates interested participants.
- The guide takes responsibility for the on-site program; participants may share transport, pay clear entry/travel fees and offer an optional donation to the guide.
- Work answers ordinary questions, keeps capacity under control, sends reminders where reachable, and collects post-event feedback.
- Members never have to change platform, fill external forms, or use email.

## Non-goals for first pilot
- Real-time response or guaranteed availability 24/7.
- Automatic charging, payments, phone calls or WhatsApp.
- A new webapp or Facebook scraping/API-based bot.
- Unverified automatic decisions about an organizer's identity or suitability.
- Publishing personal attendee data outside Facebook.

## Evaluation after first two excursions
- Did Messenger + Cloud Browser work end-to-end?
- How many Work interventions/approvals and failed actions occurred?
- How much Work capacity was used?
- Did people volunteer to lead, and how many registered participants attended?
- Were Facebook-only communications easy for both guides and attendees?
